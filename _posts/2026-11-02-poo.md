## P.O.O

### Overview

P.O.O is a small Active Directory PRO LAB from HackTheBox that puts enumeration, lateral movement and privilege escalation to the test. The goal is to compromise the perimeter host, escalate privileges and ultimately take over the domain, collecting several flags along the way.

The lab touches on:

- IIS short name / `.DS_Store` enumeration
- MSSQL linked server abuse
- `SeImpersonatePrivilege` exploitation
- IPv6 pivoting
- Kerberoasting
- Active Directory ACL abuse

It's a nice, quick challenge if you want to get a feel for chaining MSSQL links into a full domain compromise.
